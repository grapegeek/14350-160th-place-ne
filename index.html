The property

A small farm twenty minutes from Bellevue.

A 1976 mid-century home on 2.3 open acres, with Olympic Mountain views, a 1,786 sq ft barn and about an acre and a half of pasture. The house has been updated top to bottom: a complete kitchen remodel and refinished floors in 2023, then a new 30-year roof and gutters, a Trex deck, fresh exterior paint and a five-bedroom septic system in 2026. Every window has been upgraded, and the house has been pre-inspected.

It sits in Hollywood Hill's rural-area zoning, where lots this size are rarely created anymore, and among neighbors who keep horses of their own.

Room to add on

A second home on the property, possibly.

King County's rules generally allow an accessory dwelling unit (ADU), such as a backyard cottage or a separate apartment, on rural-area lots like this one. With 2.3 acres, there's room to place one well away from the main house, for a parent, an adult child, a caretaker or guests.

A new unit would need its own wastewater plan. Depending on the design, it might connect to the five-bedroom OSCAR septic system installed in April 2026, or it might need a separate system of its own.

We can't promise an ADU can be built. That depends on the plans a buyer submits and on King County's review. If an ADU matters to you, talk with King County Permitting about your plans before you make an offer.

Hollywood Hill

Country roads, city commute.
