A frosty sunrise from the deck

This was the view from our deck this morning, looking west as the sun rose behind us. The maples that line the driveway have turned vibrant yellows and reds, the field was covered in frost, and pink clouds were glowing along the horizon.

This is the kind of view you get all the time through our large windows. Every season looks different, and it never gets old.

The neighborhood horse

A couple of properties down the road lives a very friendly horse. Many of the neighborhood kids stop by on their way past to pet her nose and say hi, and she's always happy to come over to the fence to greet them.

Hollywood Hill is a great place to have horses. So many trails. So many horses. Lots of trainers and stables nearby.

Fall colors on Hollywood Hill

The fall colors are beautiful right now. We have about a dozen maple trees that line the driveway, and every fall they turn a spectacular shade of red, which makes things feel very seasonal. We look forward to it every year.

October 26, 2026The house

Twenty-three years on Hollywood Hill

We bought this house in 2003 while we were driving our first son around, trying to get him to sleep as a baby. We saw the sign at the end of the driveway, got home, looked it up on the internet and realized this was a great neighborhood and piece of land to put down roots and stay for a while. I didn't think it would be 23 years!

The things that really sold us were the big sky views, the abundance of land and the ability to do what you wanted. No HOAs. Outside the city limits. Good schools. Close to work. Another child came a year later, and we made lots of friends and enjoyed our time here. But it's time to move on, and we would love for the next people to enjoy it as much as we did.

One of our favorite things to do was watch the balloons float by on lazy summer evenings. Sometimes they even landed on our field. It's one of the unique things about living on Hollywood Hill!

Over the coming months we'll post here about the seasons on the hill, the projects we've done, and the corners of the property that photos don't capture.
